# Sparse boulder preview — awaiting local validation

`BoulderPreview.tscn` scatters up to **14** copies of the existing mossy `Boulder001_A_LOD1.gltf` inside the new **flat-side positive-Z forest**. It reuses the forest's centre, radii and reserved tree sites rather than defining its own competing biome footprint. A small gap is reserved around pine positions and the winding walkable trail. Positions and yaw are seeded, boulder scales vary 0.55–1.0, and 0.27 m terrain sink creates natural embedded bases. The scene is a child of `NatureRoot/Rocks` in `test.tscn`.

The source glTF is about 5.5 × 3.5 × 4.3 m with 5,120 triangles, one opaque PBR material, and colour/normal/ORM maps. Import-generated mesh LOD is enabled and a 190 m renderer cutoff prevents very distant drawing. The separate authored LOD2 source is **not yet switched** automatically. A single convex hull is prepared once from the imported LOD1 mesh and reused for static collision on each boulder through the asset-agnostic PHYSICS/StaticCollision helper. Its source, filtering and shape offset are defined by ASSETS/Vegetation/Rocks/Boulder001/BoulderCollision.tres, referenced by BoulderMaster.tres. Hull simplification remains off, preserving the previous collision recipe. This is simple visual/physical cover for the test, not damage or interaction. Rocks use physics **layer 3 (bitmask 4)** while the player's mask includes both terrain (1) and rocks (4), so the adapter's terrain-only raycasts never hit boulders when grounding trees or grass. F1 visibility is *visual-only* and does not turn collision off.

`Configuration/BoulderMaster.tres` controls count, spacing, scale, slope filtering, shadows, collision, and distance. Native terrain collision is sampled only as each boulder comes into player range, at three queries per physics tick maximum; missing collision retries with backoff. While unplaced candidates remain, one 0.6-second timer checks nearby loading. Once all sites place or are skipped, physics and timer stop. Boulders are static; no per-boulder scripts/updates. This small fixed set is **not** an infinite streamed rock generator or a batching solution. The Bermuda grass currently does not exclude boulder footprints, so some tufts may intersect rocks until a later foliage-masking pass.

Run `WORLD/Scenes/test.tscn` after a Godot .NET build; turn away from the original uphill trees into the flatter forest (+Z), then check different-sized rocks around the pine trunks. Test that rocks land correctly and block movement, have correct textures, don't obstruct the winding opening, and don't duplicate after F4. F1 → OTHER MODELS → `Visible: BoulderPreview` lets you isolate their visual cost with F2. Source-level checks were performed; Godot compilation, collision alignment and actual renderer/performance acceptance remain local.
